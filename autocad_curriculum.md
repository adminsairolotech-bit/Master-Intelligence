## Phase 1: The Foundation (Videos 1-5)
- **Topics**: Interface Overview, Navigation, Units (`UN`), Limits (`LIMITS`), and Grid/Snap settings.
- **Key Command**: `L` (Line), `U` (Undo).

## Phase 2: Core 2D Drafting (Videos 6-15)
- **Topics**: Circle, Polyline (`PL`), Rectangle (`REC`), Arc, and Polygon.
- **Advanced Core**: Coordinate systems (Absolute, Relative, Polar).

## Phase 3: The Modification Engine (Videos 16-25)
- **Topics**: Offset (`O`), Trim (`TR`), Extend (`EX`), Fillet (`F`), Chamfer, and Mirror (`MI`).
- **Logic**: Mastering precision editing.

## Phase 4: Annotation & Precision (Videos 26-30)
- **Topics**: Text (`T`), Dimension Styles (`D`), Leader lines, and Table creation.

## Phase 5: Layer Management (Videos 31-35)
- **Topics**: Creating Layers (`LA`), Layer states, Line types, and Colors.
- **Goal**: Professional drawing organization.

## Phase 6: Blocks & Reusability (Videos 36-40)
- **Topics**: Create Block (`B`), Insert (`I`), WBLOCK, and Design Center (`ADC`).

## Phase 7: Parametric & Arrays (Videos 41-45)
- **Topics**: Rectangular/Path/Polar Arrays (`AR`), Hatching (`H`), and Gradient fills.

## Phase 8: Advanced Drafting Layouts (Videos 46-50)
- **Topics**: Model Space vs Paper Space, Viewports (`MV`), and Plotting to PDF.

## Phase 9: Mechanical/Civil Project Labs (Videos 51-58)
- **Topics**: Drawing a multi-room floor plan, mechanical gear design, and section views.

## Phase 10: 3D Modeling Introduction (Videos 59-63)
- **Topics**: Workspace Switching, Extrude, Revolve, and 3D Visual Styles.
